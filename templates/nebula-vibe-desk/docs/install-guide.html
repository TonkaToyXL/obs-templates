Local install guide. Mac runtime: ~/Library/Application Support/OBS-Templates. Health/config: 127.0.0.1:18765.

browser source obs 30.0 local bridge
STEP 01 free / no account

Download & unzip

Get the zip from GitHub.

STEP 02 installs runtime + opens OBS

Double-click install

Mac: install.command / Windows: Install.bat

STEP 03 orb source is ready

Pick the scene

OBS > Scene Collection > Nebula Vibe Desk > Vibe Coding.

STEP 04 local diagnostics

Check health

Open http://127.0.0.1:18765/health.html to verify the bridge, OBS, mic, and meter. Open http://127.0.0.1:18765/config.html for local tuning.

STEP 05 optional

Customize your brand

Use http://127.0.0.1:18765/config.html, or replace assets/logo.png and edit branding.user.json (copy from branding.user.example.json).
