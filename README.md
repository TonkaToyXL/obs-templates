## Quick install (any template)

1. Click **Download** above
2. Unzip anywhere on your computer
3. Open the folder's `README.md` and follow the steps

## Requirements

- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) 30 or newer
- Voice-reactive templates need **WebSocket** enabled: OBS → **Settings** → **WebSocket**
